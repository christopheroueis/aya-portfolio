01

SOTTO SPINA

Site
Via Stradella, Torino
Year
2026
Type
Group Project

The project investigates the city as a system of interconnected layers, shaped by different levels, functions and relationships that define its urban structure. Located in Via Stradella, Turin, the intervention seeks to reconnect these layers while revitalizing an area that had become largely inactive and disconnected from its surroundings.

Through the reorganization of spaces and the articulation of different levels, the project introduces new functions and opportunities for interaction, transforming a neglected urban space into a lively environment.

Sotto Spina becomes a connecting element that weaves together architecture, movement and urban life, restoring continuity and giving the area a renewed sense of identity.

02

CO-WORKING SPACE

Site
Beirut, Lebanon
Year
2023
Type
Individual Project

The emergence of new workspaces in Badaro has become a recurring habit, which is why it is imperative that our designs not only meet current working needs but also pave the way for future possibilities.

In designing this project, my vision was to create a space that not only facilitates collaboration and productivity, but also evolves with the dynamic demands of its users. The module which is a rectangular structure with a central void is designed for versatility and expansion.

In today's fast-paced world, as businesses evolve, so do the spatial needs of employees. This modular design therefore allows us to respond to these requests with great flexibility.

The voids inside create a sense of community which forms the basis of a coworking environment.

By choosing modularity, we are not only building a space for today, but we are also laying the foundation for the workplaces of tomorrow.

03

ZEN HAVEN

Site
Beirut, Lebanon
Year
2024
Type
Individual Project

My interior project concerns the design of a yoga instructor's house. I paid particular attention to the different levels in order to create a feeling of proximity to the ground, in resonance with the principles of yoga, in particular the connection to the earth.

All the furniture is low, inspired by Japanese style, to promote this intimacy with the space. I opted for a natural and neutral color palette, which embodies serenity and zen.

Organic materials such as rock, gravel and wood were incorporated to reinforce this natural look.

In addition, almost all of the furniture has been custom designed, to specifically meet the needs and spirit of the place.

04

BAYNETNA - A SUSPENDED SPACE FOR DIALOGUE

Site
Beirut, Lebanon
Year
2025
Type
Group Project

Amidst the vibrant energy and chaos of Beirut, this urban parklet offers a moment of pause. Built entirely from modular scaffolding materials already present in the neighborhood, the intervention easily scales and transforms to meet evolving community needs.

By shifting streetscapes from mere thoroughfares into shared living spaces, the project seeks to reconcile the city with its inhabitants through social interaction and flexible design.
